# Open Legislative and Drafting Questions

This list distinguishes unresolved implementation details from settled policy choices. It is not an invitation to silently weaken the core rights.

## High priority

- [ ] **Set a mandatory final compliance-by date.** The sponsoring legislator and legislative counsel must select a definite date, with phased milestones for technical, operational, and administrative obligations.
- [ ] Define interim procedures that covered businesses must offer during implementation.
- [ ] Establish monitoring, reporting, and narrowly defined procedures for any deadline adjustments. No open-ended or permanent exemption.
- [ ] Obtain legislative counsel review of interactions with 17 U.S.C. §§ 106, 117, and 1201, including precise amendments, exceptions, and cross-references needed to make the proposal operative.
- [ ] Finalize federal/state enforcement coordination and any necessary preemption language without displacing stronger consumer protections.

## Definitions and regulations

- [ ] Refine the definition of a qualifying permanent purchase, including mixed bundles and promotional entitlements.
- [ ] Define compatible hardware, platform family, and true successor generation using objective technical criteria.
- [ ] Define material unavailability, essential online service, and reliable sustained restoration.
- [ ] Define reasonable interim compliance and technically feasible preservation measures.
- [ ] Clarify evidentiary standards for license ownership and transfer without requiring dependence on one storefront account.

## Enforcement and procedure

- [ ] Finalize civil penalties for repeated or willful violations.
- [ ] Set limitation periods and rules for calculating actual damages.
- [ ] Specify evidentiary presumptions and record-retention duties.
- [ ] Coordinate FTC, state attorney general, and private enforcement roles.
- [ ] Review class-action, arbitration, and Federal Arbitration Act language for legal precision and enforceability.

## Technical and operational review

- [ ] Consult preservationists, accessibility specialists, cybersecurity experts, developers, publishers, storefront operators, and consumer advocates.
- [ ] Test the backup, authentication, transfer, family-sharing, and community-server provisions against real-world architectures.
- [ ] Ensure security and privacy safeguards are not used as pretexts to defeat statutory rights, while preserving legitimate security requirements.
- [ ] Review treatment of cloud-native products and functions that inherently require remote infrastructure.

## Publication note

No mandatory final compliance date has been chosen. It must be supplied through the legislative process; it should not be guessed or represented as settled.
