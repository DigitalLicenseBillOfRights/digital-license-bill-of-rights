# Article II — Installation and Reinstallation

## Section 1. Right to install

A consumer holding a qualifying permanent license is entitled to install and use the licensed software on compatible hardware in accordance with the license entitlement and the limits expressly permitted by this Act.

## Section 2. Reinstallation and replacement

The consumer may reinstall the software after device failure, repair, replacement, operating-system change, storage replacement, or migration to another compatible device within the same protected platform scope.

A consumer may not be required to repurchase a qualifying license solely because the original device failed, was replaced, or could no longer run the software.

## Section 3. Installation materials and credentials

Covered entities shall provide reasonable means for the consumer to obtain installation files, required components, and authentication credentials necessary to exercise the license. A consumer's ability to reinstall may not depend indefinitely on the continued operation of the original storefront or a single publisher-controlled server.

## Section 4. Alternative authentication

If an authentication, activation, or entitlement-verification service becomes unavailable or is discontinued, the responsible covered entity shall provide a reasonable alternative means of verifying or exercising the consumer's entitlement, consistent with Articles IX and X.

## Section 5. No artificial device or account lockout

A covered entity may not use an account migration, storefront change, compatible hardware replacement, or administrative system change as a pretext to force repurchase or materially impair the consumer's statutory rights.

## Section 6. Implementation

Consumer rights under this Article attach immediately on the Act's effective date. A phased period may be used to implement automated systems, but reasonable interim manual procedures shall be made available where feasible. The period does not suspend the right to install or reinstall.
