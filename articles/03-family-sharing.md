# Article III — Family Sharing

## Section 1. Right to designate a family group

A consumer may designate a family group for sharing eligible qualifying permanent license entitlements, subject to the concurrency and use limitations in this Article.

## Section 2. License-based concurrency

The number of simultaneous active sessions may not exceed the number of licenses owned for the relevant software, unless the entitlement expressly permits a greater number.

For a single license, one active session is the baseline unless the purchased entitlement grants additional concurrent use. This rule does not prevent sequential use by different members of the designated family group.

## Section 3. Ownership retained

Family sharing does not transfer ownership of the license entitlement, require the owner to surrender it, or prevent the owner from ending a sharing designation in a reasonable manner.

## Section 4. Transparent administration

Covered entities shall provide transparent information about which licenses are shared, which members are authorized, and what concurrency limits apply. Restrictions must be consistent with the purchased entitlement and this Act.

## Section 5. Implementation

The right attaches on the Act's effective date. Covered entities may phase in automated entitlement systems, but shall provide reasonable interim means where feasible. Implementation delay may not be used to deny or extinguish the underlying right.
