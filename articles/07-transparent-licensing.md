# Article VII — Transparent Licensing

## Section 1. Pre-purchase disclosure

Before payment, a covered entity shall clearly and conspicuously disclose the material terms governing the software entitlement, including:

- Whether the transaction is a permanent purchase, subscription, rental, trial, or time-limited access;
- The original hardware platform and material compatibility restrictions;
- Installation and reinstallation conditions;
- Internet connectivity, authentication, and activation requirements;
- Whether offline or local operation is available;
- Family-sharing and simultaneous-use limits;
- Lending, resale, gifting, and transfer conditions;
- Essential online services and material server dependencies; and
- Any material suspension or termination conditions permitted by law.

## Section 2. Clear language

Disclosures must be understandable, prominent, and presented before purchase. A permanent purchase may not be marketed in a manner that materially misleads a reasonable consumer into believing the transaction is a subscription or revocable access arrangement, or vice versa.

## Section 3. No retroactive diminution

A covered entity may not retroactively amend an end-user license agreement, terms of service, or account policy to diminish rights attached to a qualifying permanent purchase.

## Section 4. Contract terms

Any standard-form term that conflicts with a nonwaivable right under this Act is unenforceable to the extent of the conflict.

## Section 5. Implementation

The rights attach on the effective date. A limited implementation period may be provided to revise legacy disclosures and systems, but may not be used to misrepresent an existing entitlement, obtain a waiver, or suspend a consumer right.
