# Article VI — Preservation, Offline Backup, and Continued Access

## Section 1. Personal preservation right

A consumer holding a qualifying permanent license may download, create, retain, store, restore, and use independent offline backups of installation files and components reasonably necessary to preserve and continue using the licensed software.

Permitted storage includes external drives, USB storage, optical media, network storage controlled by the consumer, and transfers among the consumer's own storage devices.

## Section 2. Versions, updates, and patches

The consumer may preserve legitimately obtained versions, updates, patches, and components associated with the qualifying license. A covered entity may not prohibit or technically obstruct reasonable personal backups solely to maintain exclusive control over distribution.

## Section 3. Backup and use are distinct

The right to retain a backup is distinct from the right to use the software. A backup may not be rendered unusable solely because an authentication, activation, license-verification, or distribution service is no longer available, where a reasonable alternative means of validating the consumer's entitlement can be provided.

## Section 4. Offline and local operation

Where technically capable, software shall remain usable in offline or local mode when a mandatory online service is unavailable or permanently discontinued. This obligation may include reasonable modification of software originally designed to require continuous connectivity.

Nothing in this section requires recreating a function that inherently depends on remote computation, live third-party data, or infrastructure that cannot reasonably be replicated locally. Legitimate security and legal requirements remain applicable and may not be invoked as a pretext to defeat the Act.

## Section 5. Intellectual property boundary

Personal preservation under this Article is limited to continued use by the license holder under the holder's own entitlement. It does not authorize public distribution, creation of additional licenses, additional users, or independent sale, rental, or sharing outside the rights established by Article VIII.

Upon permanent transfer of a license, the transferor relinquishes the entitlement and may not retain or use a functional copy under that transferred entitlement. The transfer includes the associated preservation entitlement, or the recipient shall be provided a lawful means to obtain an authorized copy. Temporary lending is governed separately by Article VIII.

## Section 6. Relationship to continuity obligations

This Article establishes direct consumer preservation rights. Article IX establishes publisher and successor continuity obligations. Article X addresses preservation resources and community continuity following material unavailability or discontinuation of essential online services. These protections are cumulative.

## Section 7. Implementation

Consumer rights attach immediately on the Act's effective date. Covered entities may phase in systems for downloads, backups, and alternative authentication, but shall provide reasonable interim preservation means where feasible.
