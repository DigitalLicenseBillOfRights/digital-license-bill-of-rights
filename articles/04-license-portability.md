# Article IV — Universal License Portability

## Section 1. Purpose

A purchased license should not become unusable merely because a storefront, distribution channel, or account-management service changes. This Article distinguishes portability within the same hardware platform from the separate question of a version for a different hardware platform.

## Section 2. Storefront portability on the same platform

A consumer's qualifying permanent license shall remain portable among lawful storefronts or distribution channels offering the same software for the same compatible hardware platform, where technically feasible.

A consumer may not be required to repurchase the same platform version solely because the original storefront closes, changes ownership, discontinues distribution, or ceases to administer the entitlement.

## Section 3. Different hardware platforms

This Article does not automatically require a publisher to create or provide a separately developed version for a genuinely different hardware platform.

If a publisher promised a cross-platform version or later grants the consumer a license for that version, the granted entitlement may not be withdrawn contrary to this Act. A platform version expressly included in the original purchase remains part of that purchase.

## Section 4. Successor generations

Portability to compatible successor generations is governed by Article V. A covered entity may not evade successor-generation rights by describing a compatible successor device as an entirely unrelated platform.

## Section 5. Original platform disclosure

Before purchase, the covered entity shall clearly disclose the original platform or platform family for which the license is supplied and any material compatibility limitation.

## Section 6. Rights preserved on transfer

Portability shall preserve, as applicable, the consumer's rights to install, reinstall, use, family-share, lend, sell, gift, transfer, and preserve the software under this Act. A change of storefront shall not silently strip those rights.

## Section 7. Anti-circumvention

A covered entity may not impose artificial technical or contractual barriers designed primarily to defeat portability rights, including requiring repurchase where a same-platform authorized version or reasonable transfer method exists.

## Section 8. Implementation

Rights attach on the effective date. Covered entities may phase in automated portability systems, but shall provide reasonable interim ownership-verification and manual portability procedures where feasible.
